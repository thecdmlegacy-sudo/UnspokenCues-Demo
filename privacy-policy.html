Privacy Policy for Unspoken Cues

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Unspoken Cues respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Unspoken Cues mobile and Wear OS applications handle information.

Information We Collect

Unspoken Cues may process information that you voluntarily provide when using features such as your profile, preferences, status/availability settings, and QR/profile features.

The app may also process basic technical information necessary for the phone and Wear OS applications to communicate and function properly.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and improve Unspoken Cues features, including displaying your selected status, providing profile functionality, enabling QR features, and allowing supported communication between your phone and Wear OS device.

Sharing of Information

Unspoken Cues does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Information you intentionally choose to make available through a profile, QR code, or sharing feature may be visible to people with whom you choose to share it.

Advertising

Unspoken Cues does not currently display third-party advertisements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Unspoken Cues. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's functionality. Users may request deletion of personal information associated with Unspoken Cues by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Unspoken Cues is not intended for children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Unspoken Cues develops. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
